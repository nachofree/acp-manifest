Return & Refund Policy (Hotel Reservations)

Effective Date: February 17, 2026

Important: Hotel reservations are services, not physical goods. “Returns” in this context mean cancellations and refunds for reservation payments.

This policy applies to bookings made through thegummibear (“we,” “us,” “our”).

1) Cancellation & Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on the rate rules of the hotel and the specific reservation you select at checkout. When you place a booking, the applicable cancellation terms for that reservation are shown during checkout and in your confirmation.

  • Free Cancellation (where offered): Some reservations allow cancellation for a full refund if canceled before the listed deadline.
  • Non-Refundable (where offered): Some reservations are non-refundable once booked.
  • Partial Refunds / Penalties (where offered): Some reservations may allow cancellation with a penalty (for example, one night’s room charge) or other restrictions.

2) 24-Hour Grace Period (If Applicable)

In certain cases, we may offer a 24-hour grace period for cancellations made shortly after booking. This grace period is only available when the hotel’s rate rules and supplier terms permit it. If available, it will be shown at checkout or in your confirmation.

3) How to Cancel

To request a cancellation, contact us with your reservation details:

  • Email: support@thegummibear.com
  • Include: reservation ID, guest name, property name, and check-in date

We will confirm whether your reservation is eligible for cancellation and what refund amount (if any) applies under the reservation’s terms.

4) Refund Method & Timing

  • Refund Method: Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method.
  • Timing: After a refund is approved, processing typically takes 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.

5) Changes (Date Changes, Name Changes, Room Changes)

Changes to an existing reservation are subject to the hotel’s rate rules and availability. In many cases, changing dates or guest details may require canceling and rebooking (which may involve penalties or non-refundable terms).

6) No-Shows & Early Departures

  • No-Show: If you do not arrive for check-in, most hotels treat the reservation as a no-show and may charge penalties (often up to the full reservation amount).
  • Early Departure: Leaving early may result in penalties or non-refundable charges, depending on the reservation terms.

7) Chargebacks & Disputes

If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact us first so we can investigate and resolve the issue quickly. Initiating a chargeback may delay resolution while the payment processor reviews the dispute.

8) Exceptions & Special Circumstances

Some hotels may offer exceptions in special circumstances (for example, severe weather, emergency, or major travel disruptions). These are evaluated case-by-case and are not guaranteed.
